In this branch of the mechanicat-oramods repository are the files for one of my OpenRA modifications, the TD 2000 mod.

What this mod (TD 2000) does  

Changes the tech-tree to work via D2K-style upgrades\
The Construction Yard, Barracks, Airstrip, Weapons Factory can get upgraded\
to unlock the production of additional units and structures.\
With a Temple of Nod or Adv. Comm. Center built the upgrade\
for unlocking hi-tech units can be researched. 

The upgrades once researched stay on permanently,\
they can be found inside the Support tab, at the top. 

There are still some units and defenses that unlock via ownership of their\
prerequisite structures, but those are few. The majority of unit production\
gets changed to make use of the upgrade system.  

This change offers a middle way between old stickytech in the originals\
and the modern method of using buildings as prerequisites for unlocking. 

Base building skins for Nod side  
Nod side structures will look different to GDI ones. The power plants have green water,\
Refinery and Silos have darker parts, The construction yard and repair facility gets the\
Nod logo stamped on them. Also the Harvester and MCV have a variant icon in the sidebar.  

Changes to existing units  
Light Tanks can carry one passenger, this change was made to better balance infantry\
mobility on ground between the two factions.\
This means when playing as Nod you can use a Light Tank to ferry around Engineers,\
or any other infantry pretty much. 

Helicopters do not re-arm on air, reverted them to classic behavior\
Helicopters will need to resupply at a Helipad once out of ammo, just like in the\
OpenRA Red Alert mod. 

Commando(and its new Nod counterpart, see below) are limited to one per player.\
Temple of Nod and the Adv. Comm. Center are limited to one per player. 

The neutral capturable tech-structure Tech Center got renamed to Observatory.\
This change is to better reflect what it actually does. 

The speed of the Nod Cargo Plane was reduced as it was delivering too fast. 

Construction Yard provides +30 Power. Infantry production does not require Power Plant. 

SAM Site prerequisite changed to Tiberium Refinery, so that it becomes available\
at same time as the Helipad becomes available for building. 

Adds multiple units for both sides  
Medic from RA backported and added to GDI. It functions identical to RA, however is much\
later in the tech-tree becoming available, near the end instead of available at begin. 

Mobile Radar (GDI) and Mobile Jammer (Nod)  
the Mobile Radar acts as stealth detector and radar, the jammer prevents radar and detection.\
The Mobile Jammer does NOT deflect missiles as it does in the OpenRA Red Alert mod.\

Megan, a female Commando equivelant unit for Nod  
She looks like a red-headed Tanya with rifle instead of pistoles, she uses Tanya voicelines.\
Stats-wise, she has slightly less hitpoints and moves faster compared to the Commando, also\
costs a little bit less to train. 

Useable wall gates  
Those 3-cell wide Barrier Gates can be used to restrict access into your base.\
Can rotate using a hotkey (default: V) to switch vertical/horizontal variant\
when placing down.
